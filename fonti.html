Sources and method

Where the numbers come from

Source

All the data come from the Missing Migrants Project of the International Organization for Migration (IOM), which records people who died or went missing while migrating.

CSV downloaded on the 16th November 2026 - data up to 9th November 2026

What we keep

Only incidents on the three Mediterranean routes, western, central and eastern, from January 2014.

That is 3,159 incidents and 35,692 people.

What we count

Victims are the confirmed dead plus those assumed to be dead.

Confirmed dead are people whose body was recovered. Assumed to be dead is the minimum number of people never found: the real number is higher.

Limits

Shipwrecks nobody witnessed are not counted. Age and sex are often not recorded. 2026 is a partial year.

Some incidents are split or grouped by the IOM itself; we keep them as they are.
