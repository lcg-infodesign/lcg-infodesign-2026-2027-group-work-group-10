What is the Missing Migrants Project?

The Missing Migrants Project of the International Organization for Migration (IOM) records people who died or went missing while migrating to another country. It started in 2014, after the shipwrecks off Lampedusa in October 2013.

This site looks only at the three Mediterranean routes, western, central and eastern. Data: IOM Missing Migrants Project, downloaded 16 November 2026, up to 9 November 2026.
